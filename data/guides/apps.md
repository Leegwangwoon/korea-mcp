# Apps that actually work in Korea

Google Maps has very limited walking/driving directions in Korea (map-data export restrictions). Locals use:

- **Naver Map** — best overall; English UI, transit + walking directions, restaurant info. Set the language in settings.
- **KakaoMap** — similar coverage, good real-time bus arrivals.
- **Kakao T** — taxi hailing. Foreign-card support has been improving; you can always pay the driver directly by card or cash.
- **Papago** (Naver) — translator tuned for Korean, including camera/menu translation.
- **Subway Korea** / **Kakao Metro** — offline subway maps and route planning.
- **Visit Korea** (KTO) — official tourism information.

Tip: many restaurants list hours and "break time" only on Naver Map. Shops often close one day a week.
