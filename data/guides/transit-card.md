# Transit cards (T-money and friends)

- **T-money** (or the compatible **Cashbee**) is the standard prepaid card for subway, buses and most taxis nationwide.
- **Where to buy:** convenience stores (GS25, CU, 7-Eleven, Emart24) and machines in subway stations.
- **Top-up:** usually **cash only** at station machines and convenience-store counters. Many foreign credit cards cannot top up — carry some ₩10,000 notes.
- **Tap in and tap out.** On buses tap when you get off too, or you lose the free transfer and may be charged the maximum distance fare.
- **Free transfers:** subway ↔ bus transfers are free (you only pay extra distance) if you tap within 30 minutes of getting off (60 minutes at night, 21:00–07:00). Up to 4 transfers per journey.
- **Single-journey tickets** cost ₩100 more than the card fare and need a ₩500 deposit that you get back at a refund machine.
- **Unlimited passes:** Seoul's Climate Card (기후동행카드) has short-term passes for visitors; it is valid on Seoul-area subway and Seoul city buses only (not Shinbundang Line or most Gyeonggi buses).
- **Refunds:** remaining balance can be refunded at convenience stores (small fee); the card price itself is not refunded.

Prices and pass rules change — check the operator's site for current amounts.
