# Incheon Airport (ICN) ↔ Seoul

**AREX (Airport Railroad)** — the easiest train.
- **All-stop train (일반열차):** stops at Gimpo Airport, Hongik Univ. (Hongdae), Gongdeok, Seoul Station and others. Pay with T-money. About an hour to Seoul Station.
- **Express train (직통열차):** non-stop Incheon Airport ↔ Seoul Station, about 45 minutes. Needs its own ticket (counter, kiosk or app) — T-money and Climate Card are not accepted.
- Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 have separate stations; check which terminal your airline uses.

**Airport limousine buses** go directly to many hotels and districts (Myeongdong, Gangnam, Jamsil…). Good with heavy luggage; buy tickets at the counters in the arrivals hall.

**Taxis** — use the official taxi rank outside arrivals. Expressway tolls are added to the meter. Late night (22:00–04:00) has a surcharge.

**Gimpo Airport (GMP)** is mostly domestic plus some short-haul (Tokyo Haneda, Osaka, Shanghai, Taipei). It is on Line 5, Line 9, AREX, Seohae Line and the Gimpo Goldline.
