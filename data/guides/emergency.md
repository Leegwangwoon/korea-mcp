# Emergency & help numbers in Korea

| Number | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **112** | Police | Interpretation available; say the language you speak. |
| **119** | Fire, ambulance, medical emergency | Ambulance is free. Also gives medical guidance by phone. |
| **1330** | Korea Travel Hotline (Korea Tourism Organization) | 24/7, multilingual (English, Japanese, Chinese and more). Can interpret calls with taxis, shops, hospitals. Add area code from a foreign SIM: +82-2-1330. |
| **1345** | Immigration Contact Center | Visas, Alien Registration Card (ARC), stay extensions. Multilingual. |
| **120** | Dasan Call Center (Seoul) | Seoul city services, with foreign-language support. |

Tips
- From a foreign phone, emergency numbers 112/119 work without a Korean SIM.
- Pharmacies (약국, "yak-guk") handle minor illness; many close at night and on Sundays. Look for "휴일지킴이 약국" (holiday-duty pharmacy).
- Large hospitals have International Healthcare Centers with English-speaking staff.
